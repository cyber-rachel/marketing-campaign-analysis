# Marketing Campaign Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes **200,000 marketing campaign records** to evaluate campaign reach, engagement, conversion performance, acquisition costs, and return on investment (ROI).

The analysis was developed in Microsoft Excel using data preparation, PivotTables, calculated metrics, time-based analysis, visualizations, and an executive-style dashboard.

The objective is to identify performance patterns across **campaign types, marketing channels, locations, target audiences, and weekly trends**, and translate those findings into practical marketing recommendations.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis explores questions such as:

- How are impressions and clicks changing over time?
- What is the overall conversion rate and acquisition cost?
- How is ROI performing compared with the previous period?
- Which locations generate the strongest ROI?
- Which marketing channels generate the greatest reach and return?
- Which campaign types perform best?
- Which target audiences account for the greatest campaign reach?
- What areas should marketers prioritize for optimization?

## Analysis

The analytical workbook examines campaign performance from several perspectives.

### Weekly Performance

Weekly analysis compares:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Conversion Rate
- Acquisition Cost
- ROI

The analysis also calculates period-over-period changes to identify movement in campaign performance.

![Marketing Campaign Analysis](images/analysis.png)

### Geographic Performance

ROI is compared across:

- Chicago
- Houston
- Los Angeles
- Miami
- New York

Miami records the highest displayed ROI at approximately **$201.84K**, while Houston records the lowest at approximately **$199.04K**.

### Channel Performance

Marketing channels are evaluated using impressions and ROI.

The displayed analysis shows **Website** leading the channel comparison for impressions and ROI.

### Campaign Type Performance

Campaign types are compared across:

- Email
- Display
- Search
- Influencer

Email records the highest displayed impressions at approximately **19.08M** and the highest displayed ROI at approximately **$17.16K**.

### Target Audience

Campaign reach is also examined across target-audience segments.

The **Men 18–24** segment records approximately **221.23M impressions** in the selected analysis view, making it an important segment for further investigation.

## Key Insights

### 1. ROI increased substantially

ROI increased by approximately **109.27%** compared with the comparison period, while conversion rate and acquisition cost remained relatively stable.

This suggests that the improvement in ROI warrants further investigation into the factors contributing to stronger returns.

### 2. Miami leads displayed location ROI

Miami records the highest displayed ROI at approximately **$201.84K**.

The relatively narrow difference between locations suggests that geographic performance is broadly consistent, although Miami provides a small performance advantage.

### 3. Website is a leading channel

Website records approximately **16.21M impressions** and leads the displayed channel ROI comparison.

This makes Website an important channel to continue evaluating within the overall marketing mix.

### 4. Email leads campaign-type performance

Email records approximately **19.08M impressions** and approximately **$17.16K ROI**, leading the displayed campaign-type comparison on both measures.

### 5. Men 18–24 represents substantial campaign reach

The Men 18–24 target audience records approximately **221.23M impressions** in the selected analysis view.

Further analysis could determine which campaigns, channels, and locations are contributing most to this reach.

### 6. Reach and traffic increased at a similar rate

Impressions increased by approximately **9.28%**, while clicks increased by approximately **9.23%**.

The similar growth rates indicate that increased campaign exposure was accompanied by a broadly consistent increase in traffic.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Prioritize high-performing Email campaigns**  
   Email leads the displayed campaign-type comparison for both impressions and ROI.

2. **Continue evaluating Website performance**  
   Website leads the displayed channel metrics and should remain an important part of the marketing mix.

3. **Investigate the drivers of the ROI increase**  
   The 109.27% period-over-period ROI increase should be investigated to identify strategies that can potentially be replicated.

4. **Analyze the Men 18–24 segment further**  
   Examine the campaigns, channels, and locations contributing to the segment's substantial reach.

5. **Review Houston campaign performance**  
   Investigate why Houston records the lowest displayed location ROI and identify potential location-specific optimization opportunities.
